Design, build and launch real websites for real clients, and leave ready for a junior developer role or university.

Course Overview

This two-year course is for anyone who wants a career building websites and digital products. You will work in our digital studios at City Centre Campus on briefs set by local employers, building a portfolio you can show to employers and universities.

You will be taught by industry-experienced tutors and use the same tools professional web teams use every day.

What you will learn

  • Front-end development with HTML, CSS and JavaScript
  • Responsive and accessible web design (WCAG)
  • Back-end fundamentals, databases and APIs
  • User experience (UX) and interface design
  • Testing, version control and deployment
  • Working to client briefs as part of a team

Entry Requirements

Typically 5 GCSEs at grade C or above, including English or Welsh first language and Maths, or a relevant Level 2 qualification. If you are unsure whether you meet the requirements, our advice team will help.

Fees & funding

Full-time courses are free for learners aged 16–18. You may also be eligible for help with travel, equipment and the Education Maintenance Allowance (EMA).

Where it can lead

  • Junior web developer
  • Front-end developer
  • Digital content and web support roles
  • Higher education, e.g. a Foundation Degree or HND in computing

How to apply

  1. 1Apply onlineComplete our short online application.
  2. 2Meet usCome to an informal interview or taster session.
  3. 3Get your offerReceive your offer and enrol in the summer.

Trosolwg y Cwrs

Mae’r cwrs dwy flynedd hwn ar gyfer unrhyw un sydd eisiau gyrfa yn adeiladu gwefannau a chynhyrchion digidol. Byddwch chi’n gweithio yn ein stiwdios digidol yng Nghampws Canol y Ddinas ar friffiau gan gyflogwyr lleol, gan greu portffolio i’w ddangos i gyflogwyr a phrifysgolion.

Byddwch chi’n cael eich addysgu gan diwtoriaid sydd â phrofiad o’r diwydiant ac yn defnyddio’r un offer y mae timau gwe proffesiynol yn eu defnyddio bob dydd.

Beth fyddwch chi’n ei ddysgu

  • Datblygu pen blaen gyda HTML, CSS a JavaScript
  • Dylunio gwe ymatebol a hygyrch (WCAG)
  • Hanfodion pen ôl, cronfeydd data ac APIs
  • Profiad defnyddwyr (UX) a dylunio rhyngwynebau
  • Profi, rheoli fersiynau a chyhoeddi
  • Gweithio i friffiau cleientiaid fel rhan o dîm

Gofynion Mynediad

Fel arfer 5 TGAU gradd C neu uwch, gan gynnwys Saesneg neu Gymraeg iaith gyntaf a Mathemateg, neu gymhwyster Lefel 2 perthnasol. Os nad ydych chi’n siŵr a ydych chi’n bodloni’r gofynion, bydd ein tîm cynghori yn eich helpu.

Ffioedd a chyllid

Mae cyrsiau llawn amser am ddim i ddysgwyr 16–18 oed. Efallai y byddwch chi hefyd yn gymwys i gael help gyda theithio, offer a’r Lwfans Cynhaliaeth Addysg (LCA).

Ble gall arwain

  • Datblygwr gwe iau
  • Datblygwr pen blaen
  • Swyddi cynnwys digidol a chymorth gwe
  • Addysg uwch, e.e. Gradd Sylfaen neu HND mewn cyfrifiadura
